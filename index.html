A note from the neighbours —

This building's been here longer than any of us. Arson took the roof. It didn't take the Bull. We're bringing it back — market, music, a love-lock wall, and a beam of light through the open sky where the roof used to be. £5 a lock. 200 people at £50 and we own a piece of the High Street.

Come down. Bring someone. Tell somebody. The only pub in Yorkshire with a skylight you didn't ask for.
