200 years.One fire. Not done.

The story of why a boarded-up Victorian pub on a South Yorkshire high street is about to become one of the most interesting community projects in the country — and why we're asking you to stand with it.

Written by Earth Village CIC · April 2026 · Mexborough, South Yorkshire

The building

The Bulls Head has stood on Mexborough High Street since 1878. It outlived two world wars, the fall of the pits, three generations of landlords, and the slow emptying of the town centre that every British working-class high street knows by heart. For most of that time it was a pub — the kind where the beer was cheap, the carpet was sticky, the back room held a funeral and a wedding in the same week.

In 2024, it was set on fire. The roof went. The inside went. The windows got boarded up. And for a long year, it sat there — scorched, silent, and staring out at the £40 million regeneration of the borough happening around it.

"200 years old. Burned by arson. Left for dead. But this pub's not done yet."

Justin

Justin Harris still owns the building. He didn't flip it. He didn't sell it for flats. He sat tight through the fire, through the insurance fights, through the boarding-up, and waited for something worth doing. When Earth Village CIC walked through the door with a plan for a community hub — not a pub — he said yes.

That's rare. In most towns, a building like this gets bought by a developer, stripped of anything heritage-worthy, and turned into a block of flats you can't afford. The Bulls Head gets a different ending because Justin said so.

Earth Village CIC

Earth Village isn't new to this. For years they've been running gatherings — off-grid, phone-free, all-ages — where people rediscover what community actually feels like. The festivals are the proof-of-concept. The Bulls Head is the next step: take that feeling off the fields and drop it onto a high street, year-round, walk-in, free at the point of use.

The vision

The Bulls Head, rebuilt and reopened, will be:

Youth club
Sharing circles
Yoga / wellbeing
Multi-faith space
Parkour / movement
Alcohol-free nights
Live music
Markets
Meditation
Community café

All of this, in one building, at the centre of a town that right now has nowhere to do any of it. Free at the point of use wherever possible. Pay-what-you-can where it isn't. No one priced out.

"The only pub in Yorkshire with a skylight you didn't ask for."

The money

Let's be straight about this. Here's roughly what the building needs, before it can do any of the above:

Roof stabilisation + temporary cover~£12,000
Structural survey + listed-building checks~£3,000
Clear-out + emergency electrics~£5,000
Opening weekend — market, lock wall, Bull Signal~£2,500
Year-1 programming budget (speakers, youth sessions, events)~£18,000
Community-ownership legal structure (CBS setup)~£1,500
Phase 1 total target£42,000

We're applying for £16,500 through the Architectural Heritage Fund. We're asking the town for love-locks (£5 each), beam sponsors (£50), and founder-wall plaques (£500). And we'll issue community shares — one person, one vote — so the building's ownership never leaves Mexborough.

Every penny goes on the building and its programme. Not a single salary comes out of grant money until the hub is actually running.
