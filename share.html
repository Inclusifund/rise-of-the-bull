A derelict Victorian pub on Mexborough High Street is about to become one of the most interesting community projects in South Yorkshire. The Bulls Head, boarded since a 2024 arson, has been picked up by Earth Village CIC in partnership with the building's owner, Justin Harris. The plan: not another pub. A multi-faith, alcohol-free community hub — youth club, wellbeing space, live music venue, market — under the open sky where the roof used to be. Right in the heart of the borough's £40m regeneration zone. If you're in Yorkshire social enterprise, community ownership, or built-environment work — this is worth a look. Door-knock script · 50 words · 30 seconds

"Hiya — I'm not selling anything. I live round here. There's a community project to bring the Bulls Head back as a hub — youth stuff, markets, events, no booze required. They're asking the town what it wants. 3-minute survey, no money, just your voice. Can I text you the link?"

Then: text the link straight away. Don't leave it to "later". Cross it off, move to the next door. Aim for 10 doors in an hour.
